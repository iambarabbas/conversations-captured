What is a Legacy Session?

A Legacy Session is a simple filmed conversation designed to preserve a person — not a performance.

Not a scripted interview.
Not a production set.

Just a comfortable, guided conversation that becomes something your family can keep and return to for years.

Each session includes

  • Up to 2 hours of in-home filming (San Diego County)
  • A relaxed, guided conversation
  • A lightly edited legacy film
  • 10–20 short clips you can share with family or keep privately
